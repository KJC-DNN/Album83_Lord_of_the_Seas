# Album 83 Lord of the Seas
Christ remains Lord of the seas (Psalm 95:5; Matthew 8:27)

He is also Lord of every land, pole, ruin, and frequency.

## Song 6 The Pattern Screamer and the Starless Abyss
Range: SCP 3901–4000 (close of Series IV)

Every statue, sand, temple, feed, and plan bows to His Name.

Song 6 of Album 83 covers SCP 3901–4000 (Series IV close): official files (Pattern Screamer, Fata Morgana, Necrocapitalism, Bigger Than God, 3999) plus overlays from Mexico to Samoa and a named list of present-age disputes. Intercession only. Child safety is prayed as protection, not spectacle.

## Study Note
Official SCP-3930 is the Pattern Screamer (a hole in being that “screams” by absence). SCP-3933 is titled Bigger Than God — mapped as a boast to refuse. SCP-3999 places a self at the center of all events — refused in favor of Christ at the center. SCP-3914 Necrocapitalism rhymes with greed songs 3–4. Anunnaki, Vedic curse, weeping statues, and headline clusters are overlays for prayer, not manuals. Psalm 95:5 / Matthew 8:27 close the set. For anyone in crisis after hard headlines: seek local help; in the US, 988.

## Prayer
Lord Jesus, You made the sea. Bind 3901–4000 and every overlay named. Silence the screamer. Guard the children. Expose every lie. Amen.
