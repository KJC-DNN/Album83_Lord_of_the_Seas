# Song 1 The Lap That Could Not Be Erased (3401–3500)
Range: SCP 3401–3500

Song 1 of Album 83 covers SCP 3401–3500 and fuses IndyCar-style velocity used as memory-erasure, space-science manipulation, organized-crime reality-bending (named only), Strait of Hormuz choke-point intercession, and the Daniel–Rome–modern den–slug reversal. Official files in-range are named for banishing. Christ remains Lord of the seas (Psalm 95:5; Matthew 8:27).

## Study Note
Psalm 95:5 — the sea is His because He made it. Matthew 8:27 — even winds and sea obey Him. Daniel 6 is reversal, not spectacle. Rome turned the den into entertainment; Christ turns death into victory. Hormuz is a historical choke-point of empire and oil; it is not lord. Memory belongs to the Book of Life, not to speed.
