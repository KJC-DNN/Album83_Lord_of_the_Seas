# Album 83 — Lord of the Seas | SCP 3401–4000 Intercession | KJC-DNN

Seven-song worship cycle under the confession that Christ is Lord of the seas (Psalm 95:5; Matthew 8:27). Ranges SCP 3401–4000 are named for intercession only: velocity and memory, stolen hymns and false pasts, tides of war, bought hours, the voice behind, the pattern-scream, and the False Eden of stolen names. Official wiki titles are distinguished from prayer overlays. No ritual instruction. Jesus is Saviour and Victorious.
