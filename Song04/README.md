# Album 83 Lord of the Seas



## Song 4 Smiting on a Budget — The Tide That Cannot Be Bought
Range: SCP 3701–3800

Christ remains Lord of the seas and of all (Psalm 95:5; Matthew 8:27).

The love of money is a root of all kinds of evil (1 Timothy 6:10).

Song 4 of Album 83 covers SCP 3701–3800: official files (time-timer, krakens, rising tide, smiting on a budget, half-world city, false air-god, grandparents-ago) plus overlays of Wall Street / London greed, shadow exchange, spectral serpent, blood covenant, and land-tides from India to Antarctica. Algorithm greed-and-conspiracy feeds are named only for binding.

## Study Note
Official SCP-3701 is a temporal timer (“all the time in the world”) — mapped to Wall Street’s worship of the hour, not as a how-to. SCP-3720 is titled Smiting on a Budget. SCP-3711 is Suffering on the Rising Tide. SCP-3706 names krakens under the sea — Christ remains Lord of that deep. Blood covenant in Scripture is Christ’s blood (Luke 22:20), not a market seal. 1 Timothy 6:10 frames greed; Psalm 95:5 / Matthew 8:27 close the set.

## Prayer
Lord Jesus, the sea is Yours. Time is Yours. Bind 3701–3800 and every overlay named. Break the false exchange. Still the bought tide. Amen.
